# ATHI WATER WORKS DEVELOPMENT AGENCY

## ICT DEPARTMENT

# Incident Report

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0623-001  
**Date:** 23 June 2026  
**Reported By:** ICT Department  
**Severity Level:** High  
**Subject:** Organization-Wide Internet Connectivity Outage

## 1. Incident Summary

On 23 June 2026 at 10:17 a.m., Athi Water Works Development Agency experienced a complete loss of internet connectivity affecting all departments.

Both wired desktop computers and the office Wi-Fi network displayed **"No Internet Access."**

Staff were unable to access external websites, cloud-based applications, and email services. However, internal applications, including local file sharing, remained operational.

The issue was resolved at **3:42 p.m.** after the ICT Department restarted the gateway switch and firewall following confirmation that the Internet Service Provider (ISP) had repaired the damaged fiber connection.

## 3. Affected Systems and Users

### Affected Systems

- Internet connectivity services
- Corporate email services
- Cloud-based applications
- External websites
- Online collaboration platforms
- Office Wi-Fi network

### Unaffected Systems

- Internal Local Area Network (LAN)
- Local file sharing services
- Internal business applications

### Affected Users

Approximately **150 staff members** across all departments.

## 4. Business Impact

- Staff were unable to access internet-based services.
- Communication with external stakeholders was interrupted.
- Cloud-hosted applications and email services were unavailable.
- Business processes dependent on internet connectivity were delayed.
- Staff productivity was reduced.

## 5. Investigation

The ICT Department confirmed that:

- Internal network connectivity was functioning normally.
- Core switches, servers, and internal applications remained operational.
- Only external internet connectivity was affected.
- The issue originated outside the Agency's internal network.

The ISP confirmed that its primary underground fiber-optic trunk line had been damaged.

## 6. Root Cause

A road construction crew accidentally severed the ISP's primary underground fiber-optic trunk line, resulting in a complete loss of internet connectivity.

## 7. Resolution

The ISP repaired the damaged fiber-optic cable.

After confirmation that connectivity had been restored, the ICT Department restarted the gateway switch and firewall.

Internet services were restored at **3:42 p.m.**, and all systems were tested successfully.

## 8. Recommendations

1. Procure a secondary Internet Service Provider.
2. Implement automatic internet failover.
3. Conduct periodic network resilience testing.
4. Review disaster recovery and business continuity procedures.
5. Establish communication with utility and road construction contractors.
