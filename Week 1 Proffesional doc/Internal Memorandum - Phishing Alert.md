# Internal Memorandum

**Date:** 23 June 2026

**To:** All Staff

**From:** ICT Department

**Subject:** Alert on Increased Phishing Emails Impersonating Human Resource Communications

---

## Background

The ICT Department has recently detected an increase in phishing emails targeting staff members. These emails falsely appear to originate from the Human Resource (HR) Department and often contain messages regarding promotions, salary adjustments, staff benefits, or other employment-related matters.

These emails are fraudulent and are intended to trick employees into revealing sensitive information or clicking malicious links.

---

## What to Look For

Staff are advised to be vigilant and watch out for emails that:

- Claim you have received a promotion or salary increment without prior communication.
- Request immediate action or create a sense of urgency.
- Ask for usernames, passwords, or other confidential information.
- Contain suspicious links or unexpected attachments.
- Come from unfamiliar or misspelled email addresses while pretending to be from the HR Department.

---

## What You Should Do

If you receive a suspicious email:

- Carefully verify the sender's email address.
- Report the email immediately to the ICT Department.
- Contact the Human Resource Department directly if you are unsure whether the communication is genuine.
- Delete the email after reporting it.

---

## What You Should NOT Do

Please do not:

- Click on suspicious links.
- Open unexpected attachments.
- Reply to suspicious emails.
- Share your password or any confidential information through email.
- Forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

---

## Reporting Suspicious Emails

All suspected phishing emails should be reported immediately to the ICT Department for investigation and appropriate action.

If you receive a suspicious email or require assistance, please contact the ICT Department through your immediate supervisor or visit the ICT office.

Your cooperation is essential in protecting the Agency's information systems and maintaining a secure ICT environment. Thank you for remaining vigilant and following these security guidelines.
