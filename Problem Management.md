# Problem Management

## What is Problem Management?

Problem Management is an ITIL 4 practice focused on identifying and managing the root causes of incidents. While Incident Management restores service quickly, Problem Management seeks to prevent incidents from recurring.

The practice involves analyzing trends, investigating recurring issues, documenting known errors, and implementing long-term solutions.

## Why Does It Matter?

Recurring incidents consume time and resources. By identifying the underlying causes of problems, organizations can reduce downtime, improve service quality, and increase operational efficiency.

Problem Management promotes continuous improvement by addressing issues before they become major disruptions.
